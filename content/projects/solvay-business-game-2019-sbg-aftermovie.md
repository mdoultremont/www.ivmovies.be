The Solvay Business Game is designed to reveal & develop individuals capable of making a difference in business, now and in the future.

### REVEALING

We aspire to bridge the gap between theory in the classroom & companies reality by confronting students with living business questions and creating a dramatized business environment around them. We intentionally make the business challenges more real. Time is scrutinized. Problems more complex. Judges with higher standards. Pressure is higher.

Our goal is to reveal a true passion for business in students.
