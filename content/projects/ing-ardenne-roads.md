A regularity section for sports cars and vintage cars built before 1969, the ING Ardennes Roads also offers a magnificent asphalt route around Spa, the cradle of auto sport, with its long, winding and picturesque routes across the Ardennes. A classic of the calendar, this test perpetuates the tradition of the "gentlemen drivers" of yesteryear, their sporting spirit, conviviality and courtesy.
